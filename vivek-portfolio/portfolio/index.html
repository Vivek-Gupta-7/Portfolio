01 · About

Learning by building, one problem at a time.

I'm a B.Tech student currently in my 7th semester, carrying a CGPA of 8.93. Somewhere between lecture halls and late-night debugging sessions, I found the thing I actually enjoy: taking a messy real-world problem and turning it into software that people use.

That's shown up in a lot of different rooms — a hackathon team building an AR viewer, an MSME internship writing Java from scratch, a restaurant owner who paid me for a website, a shop owner who needed to stop losing track of customer credit. Academic projects taught me the theory; client work and internships taught me the rest.

I lead when I can — two years running an NSS unit — and I keep showing up to hackathons, competitions, and internships because that's where the fastest learning happens.
